Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Who controls your data?

Michał Stęchły, based in Poland, is the data controller for the Quantum Open Source Mentorship Program (QOSMP). Contact: michal@mustythoughts.com.

What we collect and why

We collect the information you provide when applying to or participating in the program, including contact details, background and experience, availability, relevant links and submissions, referral information, and any optional documents or comments you provide.

We use this information only to administer the program, assess applications and screening tasks, contact applicants, and match mentees with mentors. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in selecting participants and running the requested mentorship program (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

Providing information is voluntary and is not a statutory or contractual requirement. However, we cannot consider an application or task submission without the required fields. A CV is optional. Please do not include sensitive personal data that we have not requested.

Who receives your data?

  • Program organizers may access all three forms.
  • Mentors may access mentee applications and screening-task submissions to assess applicants and make matches.
  • Mentor applications are accessible only to program organizers.
  • Google hosts the forms and responses under its Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your data or use it for marketing.

International transfers

Google may process data outside the European Economic Area. Google describes the locations and safeguards it uses, including adequacy frameworks and European Commission standard contractual clauses, in its data transfer framework information.

How long do we keep it?

We delete application and form-submission data no later than 12 months after the relevant cohort ends.

Names of other people

Referral and prior-mentor names are optional. Before naming another person, get their permission and send them this notice. If an applicant names you, we receive your name, the referral channel or your connection to the program from that applicant. We use it only to understand the referral or prior connection, share it only with the recipients described above, and apply the same retention period and rights.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, erase, or restrict the use of your data. You may also object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact us at the email above. These rights may be limited where the GDPR permits. You may lodge a complaint with Poland's supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), or with your local EEA supervisory authority.

How decisions are made

Organizers and mentors assess applications and tasks. We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing or profiling.
